Scope Scope
The scope of this Standard is to mandate a defined national list of informational outcomes for use in the ADASTRA system across all OOH services in NHS Wales. This list is defined in the Information Specification section below.

Note – Only primary and secondary outcomes can be recorded, and these are what will be used in reporting. However, a free text field will be available on Adastra where additional information can be recorded for future reference.
