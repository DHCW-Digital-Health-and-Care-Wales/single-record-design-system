Introduction

The Welsh Government’s guidance for healthcare practitioners on working effectively with
Gypsies and Travellers, entitled ‘Travelling to Better Health’ identifies the need to collect
information relating to the health status of this ethnic category.

The White Gypsy/Irish Traveller ethnic category was included for the first time in the Office
for National Statistics 2011 Population Census.

The adoption of the 2011 Census values for ethnic category is therefore key to achieving the
requirement for “continued collection of data” and, in turn, ensuring compliance with the
objectives set out within this guidance.

Furthermore, the guidance states that “All LHBs and Trusts to have engaged with Welsh
Government-led developments to introduce Census 2011 ethnic categories into appropriate
local operational systems and central reporting.” This is a clear statement in support of the
need for this change.

In addition, this requirement formed a key element of the Welsh Government’s NHS Outcome
Framework1 for 2016-17, where a number of performance measures are dependent on the
collection of information that demonstrates a commitment to improving the health and
wellbeing of vulnerable groups, which include Gypsies and Irish Travellers.

Description of Change

Adopt the Office for National Statistics 2011 Population Census list of ethnic categories.

This amended DSCN is intended to provide clarity in relation to the value sets applicable to
the associated national data sets, stating that the value set described in Appendix B below
should be used in all relevant patient-level data sets.

Data Dictionary Version

Where applicable, this DSCN reflects changes introduced by DDCN and/or DSCN since the
release of version 4.9 of the NHS Wales Data Dictionary.

The changes introduced by such DSCNs will be published in version 4.10 of the NHS Wales
Data Dictionary.

Actions Required

Local Health Boards / Trusts:

         Health Boards update their systems and reporting processes to enable the collection
          and submission of the 2011 Census ethnic categories with effect from April 2017.

NHS Wales Informatics Service:

         Update the relevant infrastructure to enable the collection, storage, onward
          distribution and analysis of all affected data sets.
         Ensure any affected national applications managed by The Informatics Service, are
          updated.
