Appendix A –Changes to Marital Status
The shaded text is a copy of the definition for Marital Status as currently held in the NHS Wales
Data Dictionary. Changes to the definition as a result of this DSCN are highlighted in red to
denote an addition, with deletions marked with a strikethrough.

Deleted values will remaining in the definition but will be marked with a strikethrough and with
the date of deletion shown.


Marital Status

(APC ds99 –Mandatory for Psychiatric Patients Only)

Only collected for patients under the care of a consultant in a psychiatric specialty. This is the
legal status of a patient in respect of marriage. This is the legal marital or civil status of a
patient.

Format: 1 digit numeric character alpha-numeric

1               Single (Retired 01/11/2006)
